I was born in Trondheim, Norway in 1982. I grew up in a family of artists during a period when computers were rapidly becoming part of ordinary life. I was curious early, and I have been chasing that curiosity ever since.

Over the years I’ve worked on product teams, mobile safety tools, games, developer experience, visual programming, and cross-platform creative software. The details live in the work itself; what connects it all is a fondness for systems that people can actually use and enjoy.
