I'm Andreas, a Norwegian software engineer and electronic musician. I've been making things with computers since the late 90s, and have accumulated a fairly eclectic collection of interests and competencies in the years since.

Professionally I've worked on everything from graphics and animation tools to compilers, application frameworks and large enterprise systems for clients huge and tiny. Outside of work I spend a lot of time thinking about language, programming, games, music, media and computer culture. I'm concerned about it all.

This is a place for some of my work, some of my writing, and things I think are worth preserving or sharing. I'm not sure yet. It's a place.

Have a look around.
