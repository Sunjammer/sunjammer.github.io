I've been making electronic music since around 2001, mostly in the vicinity of hardcore, industrial, and breakcore. I came out of tracker culture and still have an enduring fascination with sampling, distortion, and the peculiar things that happen when sound is put through processes it was not intended for.

Making music has remained a fairly constant part of my life, irrespective of what else has been going on in it.
