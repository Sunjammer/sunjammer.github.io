Navigate the metric canvas

World coordinates are metres. X runs right; Y runs up. Zoom changes the view, not the dimensions of the space.

Create a circle
Choose Circle in the toolbar, or hold C and left-click. The circle is centered under the pointer; its diameter matches the current scale bar (10 m scale → 10 m diameter). Release C to return to navigation. Circles keep their world-space size.
Brush (B)
Drag to draw a freehand stroke; release to finish. A click makes a dot. Escape cancels an unfinished stroke. Width starts at 3 screen pixels, then stays fixed in world metres.
Path (P)
Click to place vertices. Move the pointer to preview the next segment. Enter or Finish commits an open path. Click within 10 pixels of the first vertex, or use Shift+Enter / Close, to close a polygon outline after placing at least three vertices. Backspace removes the last draft vertex; Escape cancels. Polygons are outlines, not fills.
Pan (V)
In pan mode, drag with the left button. Middle-button drag and arrow keys also pan while the path tool is selected. Choose Pan for touch pinch navigation.
Zoom
Scroll around the pointer; use + / − around the view center.
Trackpad
Shift + scroll to pan; pinch to zoom.
Touch
Drag to pan; use two fingers to pan and pinch.
Origin
Press 0 or Home to reset the view to 100 pixels per metre.

Circles and strokes are stored in memory for this page only. Switching tools discards unfinished drawing; committed objects remain. Reloading clears the scene. Selection, moving, deletion, and saved plans are not implemented yet.
